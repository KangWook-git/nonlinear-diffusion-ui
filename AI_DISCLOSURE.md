# AI-Assistance Disclosure

Development of this research software involved substantial AI-assisted work, including code generation, refactoring, debugging support, documentation, interface organization, and conceptual cross-checking.

The human author directed the modeling goals, selected and evaluated the mathematical and physical assumptions, reviewed the implementation, tested behavior during development, and remains responsible for the scientific interpretation and the released software.

AI assistance should not be interpreted as independent validation of the numerical model. Users should verify assumptions, discretization choices, parameter identifiability, boundary conditions, and numerical convergence for their own application.

This disclosure is included to make the development process transparent rather than to assign authorship to an AI system.
